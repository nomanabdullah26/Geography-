# Geography

A collection of geography study notes focused on the **physical
geography of Bangladesh**. This repository organizes key topics into
clear, structured notes for learning and revision.

## Topics Covered

### 1. Physiography of Bangladesh

An overview of the major physical regions of Bangladesh:

-   **Tertiary Hills**
    -   Low Hill Ranges
    -   High Hill Ranges
-   **Uplands / Pleistocene Terraces**
    -   Barind Tract
    -   Madhupur Tract
    -   Tippera Surface
-   **Recent Floodplains**
    -   Major River Floodplains
    -   Coastal Floodplains
    -   Other Floodplains

### 2. River Systems of Bangladesh

Notes on the major river systems and the landforms created by river
processes:

-   Bengal Basin
-   Five major river systems:
    -   Brahmaputra--Jamuna
    -   Ganges--Padma
    -   Surma--Meghna
    -   Tista
    -   Karnafuli
-   River courses: upper, middle and lower
-   River processes and landforms:
    -   Meanders
    -   Floodplains
    -   Natural levees
    -   Braided streams
    -   Oxbow lakes
    -   Deltas and the Bengal Delta
